    Notification rules (MVP)

    • Confirmation by text message with a personal status link, sent right after booking.
    • Running late: sent before arrival whenever the delay changes by 10 min or more, including the earliest sensible arrival time.
    • Coffee suggestion: shown after check-in if the expected wait is 20 min or more.
    • Come back now: sent 10 min before the earliest expected start. If the delay shrinks, it goes out sooner, so it errs on the early side.
    • Times are always shown as a range, never as an exact minute.
